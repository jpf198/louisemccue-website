Figuring It Out – Coming Soon!

When nothing else adds up, maybe you're trying to figure out the wrong thing.

Coming soon

Sarah Brewer feels completely invisible. And not in a cool superhero way.

She's a married, forty-something mum who's always struggling to get on top of her never-ending to-do list. Whether it's deciding what to cook for dinner (again), finding another spare pound for the PTFA bake sale or promising herself she'll start Couch to 5K tomorrow, she always puts everyone else first. It's no wonder no one can see who she is when she's not even sure herself.

It doesn't help that all the women around her seem to have everything figured out – the immaculate school mum, the cool, headstrong colleague and even her mum's best friend, who's ditched birdwatching in Banstead for bodyboarding in Benidorm.

Sarah hopes that landing a swanky new job in London might be just the chance she needs to rediscover a little of the confident, carefree woman she used to be. Not to mention finally being able to buy a more exciting lunch than a Greggs sausage roll on Coulsdon High Street.

What she isn't counting on, though, is her younger, cockily handsome boss seeing her as more than someone who can balance the books.

Figuring It Out is a warm, witty and uplifting novel about identity, family, friendship, love and finally putting yourself at the top of the to-do list.

Oh, and maybe a little bit of double entry.
